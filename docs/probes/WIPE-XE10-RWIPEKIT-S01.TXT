  note: /WIPE's ledger rows are 0068 (the blank arm's DISP clear, put back from the
        mode-set byte through note_restore), 006A (analog 16-colour), 007C (the GRCG mode:
        written 0xC0/0x00 every step, left at the as-found byte with the GRCG off) and, for
        W4C only, master IMR 0002 through write_byte (bit 2 cleared for the window, put back
        from the save). Its stateless writes are NOT replayed: 007E x404 per arm (the GRCG
        tile datum - left loaded and inert with 007C = 0, as the game leaves it), 0064 (the
        one-shot VSYNC arm, re-armed by wp_isr and consumed by the bounded extra edge before
        the restore) and 0000 <- 0x62 (the master specific EOI for level 2). 0xA4/0xA6 are
        left at 0 by the graphics-probe convention (their as-found bytes are in the ASFOUND
        row; they are not state this probe claims to restore). The video mode goes back with
        the BIOS's own AH=41h + AH=0Ch pair. It writes GVRAM (scribbled by design), the GRCG
        tile registers and this program's own PSP block (wp_src/wp_tab/wp_acc). INT 0Ah is
        restored and read back.
  note: 043F/0461/0463/053D read back a strap, not a mirror of the write;
        their 'now' byte cannot prove or disprove a restore.
